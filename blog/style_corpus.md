## E. Style observations (for the writer to honor, not a straitjacket)

- First-person, direct, enthusiastic; exclamation marks used sincerely, not ironically.
- Explains culture through concrete lived scenes (the grocery shop, the tea, the bazaar walk-away) and cross-cultural analogies ("like 'cuh' in the US or 'mate'").
- Comfortable saying "I" and naming what he doesn't know; states opinions plainly ("the translation is really bad").
- Casual grammar in informal register (lowercase i, occasional typos) — a blog post should read polished but keep the warmth and directness, not academic distance.
- Loves principles named by their originators (Bret Victor's immediate feedback) and connecting tools to ideas.
- Native Dari speaker from Kabul; personal connection to the material is the engine of the writing.
